The TeX Gyre files are a selection of the OpenType fonts from their distributions, which are maintained by the
GUST e-foundry. The complete works are available from the CTAN links above.

Liberation has the same metrics as Times New Roman, Arial and Courier New, and is the fallback for `strict: true`.
